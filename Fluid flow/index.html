Continuity Equation

A₁v₁ = A₂v₂ = constant

Since mass is conserved, fluid must speed up in narrower segments to maintain a constant volumetric flow rate.

Bernoulli's Principle

P + ½ρv² = constant

As the velocity of a fluid increases, the static pressure decreases, keeping the total mechanical energy constant along a streamline.
